# Blindmotion Black Friday 2026 — Creative Production Brief

## IMPORTANT REVISION — 17 NOVEMBER 2026
The original blanket **20% off everything / BF20** concept has been withdrawn before launch.

Blindmotion is using **selective Black Friday discounting**.

### Campaign headline
**BLACK FRIDAY — UP TO 20% OFF**

### Product-specific rule
- **Roller Blinds:** may explicitly say **20% OFF ROLLER BLINDS**
- **ZipGrip / Outdoor:** do **not** say ZipGrip is 20% off. Use **UP TO 20% OFF SELECTED ONLINE RANGES** or simply **BLACK FRIDAY EVENT**
- Generic/mixed creative: use **UP TO 20% OFF**, never “20% off everything”
- **Do not show BF20 or any coupon code** until Phil confirms the final pricing/coupon mechanism

## Production message
- Through 20 Nov: **Order by 20 Nov for pre-Christmas production**
- After 20 Nov: **January Production Now Open**

## Sale deadline
**Ends 30 November 2026**

## Creative principle
Product/customer proof first. Sale layer second. Protect the perceived value of differentiated products.
