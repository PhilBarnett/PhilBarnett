# Blindmotion Black Friday 2026 — Creative Production Brief

## Campaign
**BLACK FRIDAY — 20% OFF EVERYTHING ONLINE**  
**Code:** BF20  
**Production period:** January 2027  
**Public sale dates:** 27–30 November 2026 (AEDT)  
**Sale ends:** 11:59 pm AEDT, 30 November 2026

## Critical timing rule
Do **not** publish or externally expose any creative showing **20%**, **BF20** or the live Black Friday offer before **27 November 2026**.

From **21–26 November**, teaser creative may say:

**JANUARY PRODUCTION NOW OPEN**  
**BLACK FRIDAY IS COMING**

It must not reveal the discount amount or code.

## Creative strategy
Use existing approved Blindmotion footage rather than commissioning a new shoot. Prioritise:

- Koolewong ZipGrip customer-proof footage
- approved roller founder / UGC material
- Straight Drop / product footage where useful
- real factory / product / installation proof

The creative hierarchy is:

**Product/customer proof → Black Friday event → 20% offer → January production → 30 November deadline**

Keep the work feeling like genuine Blindmotion content with a decisive sale layer, not generic discount advertising.
