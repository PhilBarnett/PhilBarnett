# 05 — 21–26 Nov Black Friday Teaser / January Production 9:16

## Deliverable
Create one **9:16 teaser, approximately 10–15 seconds**, for use **21–26 November 2026 only**.

## Critical restriction
**DO NOT show 20%, BF20, discount amount or the live Black Friday offer.**

## Approved message direction
**JANUARY PRODUCTION NOW OPEN**  
**BLACK FRIDAY IS COMING**

Optional supporting line:

**Measure. Order samples. Get ready.**

## Purpose
Bridge the gap after Christmas production closes on 20 November without teaching high-intent customers to wait for a known 20% discount.

The teaser must be visually distinct enough that it cannot accidentally reveal the live Black Friday offer.
